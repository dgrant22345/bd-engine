# User Quick Start

## Get To A Useful List

1. Choose a sales or job-search workspace when you create your account.
2. Confirm your profile. Your signup details should already be filled in.
3. Add optional owners or collaborators, or continue alone.
4. Upload the unzipped LinkedIn `Connections.csv` file. Preview it before importing. You can load synthetic sample data instead.
5. Review the detected companies and keep the ones you want to track.
6. Let the first company and job-board scan run in the background.
7. Open Dashboard and work from **Today's priority queue**.

## Daily Routine

1. Review important alerts and follow-ups due.
2. Open a recommended company to see its hiring signals and contacts.
3. Choose a relevant contact and generate the appropriate message type.
4. Check the source evidence, edit the draft, and copy it.
5. After sending it in your email or LinkedIn account, log the outreach and schedule a follow-up.

## LinkedIn Import Notes

- Upload `Connections.csv`, not the ZIP archive.
- Missing optional email, title, company, or connection dates are allowed.
- Preview shows valid rows, duplicates, warnings, and selected companies before data is saved.
- Re-imports update known contacts instead of intentionally creating duplicates.
- Your notes and outreach status are preserved when matching records are refreshed.

## Hiring Coverage

BD Engine automatically imports jobs only from compatible public career feeds. A company may need review when its site blocks automated requests, renders jobs only in a browser, or uses an ATS without a public feed. One failed source does not stop other companies from refreshing.

## Need Help

Use **Support center** from the account menu in the hosted app. Include what you were doing and the approximate time. Do not paste passwords, payment details, API keys, or an entire LinkedIn export into a support request.

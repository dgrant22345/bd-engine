# 💼 Outbound Playbook for Staffing & Recruiting Agencies
*High-response outreach sequences to win client staffing contracts using real-time hiring surge signals.*

---

## 🎯 Sequence Overview: The "Hiring Surge & Stale Role" Multi-Touch Flow
- **Goal**: Reach VP of Engineering, Head of Talent, or CTO when they have open roles that are either brand new (hiring surge) or lingering (45d+ hard-to-fill).
- **Core Psychology**: Never ask "Are you hiring?". You already know they are from their public ATS. Show up with specific candidate teasers matching their active requisitions.

---

### 📩 Touch 1 (Day 1): The 10-Second Pattern Interrupt
**Channel**: Email or LinkedIn InMail  
**Subject**: `{{company}}'s open {{jobTitle}} role`  
**Body**:
```text
Hi {{firstName}},

I saw that {{company}} recently opened a search for a {{jobTitle}}{{location ? ` in ${location}` : ''}}.

Rather than sending a generic capability deck, I'll keep this brief: our team specializes in placing senior {{niche}} talent and we currently represent 2 pre-vetted candidates who are actively interviewing and match your exact stack:

• Candidate A: Senior {{niche}} Engineer (ex-{{peerCompany}}), led distributed systems scaling to 10M+ users.
• Candidate B: Lead Architect with 7+ years hands-on experience in {{primarySkill}} and modern CI/CD.

We work on pure contingency (zero retainer, 90-day replacement guarantee).

Would you be open to a 3-minute look at their anonymized profiles?

Best,
{{yourName}}
{{yourTitle}} | {{agencyName}}
```

### 📩 Touch 2 (Day 4): The "Cost of Vacancy" Value-Add
**Channel**: Email (Reply in thread)  
**Subject**: `Re: {{company}}'s open {{jobTitle}} role`  
**Body**:
```text
Hi {{firstName}},

Quick follow-up on my note from Tuesday.

I know how difficult it is to find senior {{niche}} talent in this market—every week a critical role stays open, engineering velocity takes a hit.

I put together a 2-minute compensation & market availability summary for {{jobTitle}} roles across {{locationOrRegion}} so your team has real-time benchmark data for this hiring cycle.

Happy to send the breakdown over along with the 2 candidate snapshots if you have a minute.

Best,
{{yourName}}
```

### 📩 Touch 3 (Day 8): The Low-Friction Closeout
**Channel**: Email (Reply in thread)


## ⚡ Cold Call Teleprompter (60-Second Hook)

> **You**: *"Hi {{firstName}}, I know you weren't expecting my call, but I saw {{company}} has had the {{jobTitle}} role open for over 30 days. I'll be brief—we currently have 2 pre-screened senior engineers matching your exact tech stack who are available for interview screens this week. Did I catch you at a bad time, or could I send 30-second snapshot summaries to your email?"*
>
> **Objection 1: "We only work with preferred vendors (PSL)"**  
> *"Completely respect your vendor structure, {{firstName}}. We don't need to replace your PSL—we operate exclusively as a targeted contingency carve-out for specialized roles when standard vendors stall. Zero retainer, zero risk. If our profiles don't hit the mark, you pay nothing."*
>
> **Objection 2: "Our internal team handles this"**  
> *"Your internal talent team does great work. We don't compete with inbound applicants—we bring off-market passive candidates who aren't browsing job boards. Would it hurt to see 2 profiles as a backup?"*
